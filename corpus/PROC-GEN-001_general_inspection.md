# General visual-inspection guidance

Synthetic guidance for InspectBot experiments. It is not an industry standard.

## Treat lines as hypotheses
A dark line alone does not establish a defect. Record it as a suspected anomaly and list every plausible explanation: crack, cast shadow, expected seam or joint, scratch, or contamination.

## Outcome rules
A definitive outcome needs four things:
- the target region visible in the cited observations
- an applicable, category-specific procedure
- supporting observation identifiers
- no unresolved contradiction

"No defect detected" covers only the inspected region and views. It is never a safety certification.

## When guidance is missing
General guidance is not a substitute for a category-specific procedure. If no approved procedure exists for the confirmed object category, record the missing guidance and report Insufficient evidence for human review. Do not invent a criterion.
