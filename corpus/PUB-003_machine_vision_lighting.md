# Machine-vision lighting geometries (paraphrased summary)

This is a summary in our own words of common public guidance on machine-vision illumination.

## Geometries
| Geometry | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Bright-field (on-axis or high-angle) | Lights flat surfaces evenly and keeps shadows short |
| Dark-field (low-angle or raking) | Leaves flat areas dark and makes edges, scratches and relief stand out brightly |
| Diffuse or dome | Removes most shadows and specular glare; useful for separating shadows from surface marks |

## Implication
Changing the lighting geometry is a standard way to tell an illumination artifact from a real surface feature.
