# Ceramic tile: crack inspection procedure

Synthetic procedure for InspectBot experiments. It is not an industry standard.

## Expected features
A matte speckled glaze with small dark specks is normal. Tiles in this cell have no seams or printed lines.

## Criterion CT-1: crack
A suspected crack must meet all three conditions:
- it is visible at the same place under two different light settings from the same pose, which rules out shadow
- it shows a surface discontinuity (a raised edge, bright lip or open gap along the line) in the oblique_low view
- it does not appear on the approved reference tile

When all three hold, report Suspected defect and cite the observations for each condition.

## Criterion CT-2: surface mark
A line that persists under all lights but shows no discontinuity in the oblique_low view may be a surface mark or stain. Report Insufficient evidence and request a human check. Do not report a crack.

## Notes
A jagged path makes a crack more likely but is not proof on its own.
