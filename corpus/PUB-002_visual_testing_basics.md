# Visual testing basics (paraphrased summary)

This is a summary in our own words of general public material on visual testing (VT) in non-destructive testing.

## Principles
Visual testing is the most basic non-destructive method: the inspector examines a surface directly or through a camera. Its reliability depends on:
- adequate and controllable lighting
- an angle of view that shows the area of interest
- a clean surface
- written acceptance criteria for the part being inspected

## Practice
Inspectors vary the light angle to reveal surface relief. They compare against reference samples, and they record what could not be inspected as well as what was found. A finding without a stated acceptance criterion is reported as an indication for evaluation, not as a defect.
