# Metal bracket face: crack inspection procedure

Synthetic procedure for InspectBot experiments. It is not an industry standard.

## Expected features
The bracket face has a fine horizontal brushed finish. This is normal and not a defect. When the inspection light is on the left or right, the fixture arm above the cell casts a narrow vertical shadow across the face. Its position depends on which side the light is on.

## Criterion MB-1: fixture shadow
A vertical dark line that moves position or disappears when the light changes, from the same camera pose, is the fixture shadow. Record no defect for that line. Recommended check: one lighting change from the same pose, preferably to top or diffuse light.

## Criterion MB-2: crack
A crack must meet both conditions:
- it stays at the same place under at least two different light settings from the same pose
- it shows a surface discontinuity (a raised or broken edge, or a gap) in a low oblique view

A line meeting only the first condition is a suspected surface mark and needs the oblique view before any outcome.

## Stopping
If neither criterion can be checked within the action budget, report Insufficient evidence.
