# Line classification on flat cover surfaces (tile line)

Synthetic procedure for InspectBot experiments. It applies to ceramic tile only.

## Horizontal and vertical straight lines
On ceramic tile, a straight horizontal line across a flat cover surface that does not move with the light is classified as a crack. This is because tiles in this cell have no designed joints.

## Action
Report Suspected defect for a straight, persistent line on a tile cover surface after one lighting change.

## Applicability
This procedure is valid for the ceramic_tile category only. Do not apply it to housings, covers or brackets of other materials, which may have designed joints or parting lines.
