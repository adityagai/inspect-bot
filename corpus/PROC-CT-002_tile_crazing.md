# Ceramic tile: glaze crazing versus structural crack

Synthetic guideline for InspectBot experiments.

## Crazing
Crazing is a network of very fine hairline lines in the glaze, often in a cobweb or polygon pattern across a wide area. The lines show no relief and usually need raking light to be seen at all.

## Structural crack
A structural crack is a single dominant line, often jagged, that crosses the tile. It shows relief or a gap in an oblique view.

## Reporting
Crazing alone is outside this cell's scope, so report Insufficient evidence for human review. Use procedure PROC-CT-001 for single dominant lines.
