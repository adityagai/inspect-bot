# Glass bottle: checks and cracks

Synthetic procedure for InspectBot experiments.

## What a check looks like
A check is a short, often curved or branching fracture in the glass. Under raking light it shows a bright reflective edge. It does not appear on the reference image.

## Criterion GB-3: suspected check
A line meeting all of these is a suspected check:
- it is irregular or branching, or lies off the mold-seam centre line
- it stays in place under two lights
- it shows a bright edge under raking light or in an oblique view

Report Suspected defect and cite the observations.

## Seams
Straight centre-line seams are handled by PROC-GB-001. Do not report them as checks.
